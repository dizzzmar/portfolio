Mario Diz

Mario’s taste was formed in front of Almodóvar films. An art director with three years of experience, a background in art history and a love of maximalism, he follows every idea from the first spark to the final image. In the AI team at WPP Production Madrid he blends the leading image and video AI tools with traditional craft and high-quality retouching, and he is a self-taught 3D artist.
