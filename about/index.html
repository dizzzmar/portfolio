Mario Diz

Mario’s taste was formed in museums and in front of Almodóvar films. An art director with three years of experience, he came to the craft through art history and a lasting love of maximalism, and he builds concepts and visuals across every creative route available. Curious in all the ways that matter, he follows an idea from the first spark to the final image, believing design should break the mold and question everything around it.

A tech enthusiast, Mario is fluent across the leading AI engines. He combines multiple image and video generation tools with traditional workflows, getting the speed of new technology without losing the rigor of established craft. As part of the AI team at WPP Production Madrid, he works on AI and innovation projects in advertising, and every asset goes through high-quality retouching so it arrives polished and ready for professional use. On the side, he is a self-taught 3D artist, always adding range to his toolkit.
