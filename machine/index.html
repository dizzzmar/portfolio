Mario’s taste was formed in front of Almodóvar films. An art director with three years of experience, a background in art history and a love of maximalism, he follows every idea from the first spark to the final image. In the AI team at WPP Production Madrid he blends the leading image and video AI tools with traditional craft and high-quality retouching, and he is a self-taught 3D artist.

Schweppes

Client
Schweppes
Discipline
Audiovisual / AI film
Year
2026

A film made 100% with AI, where every frame was finished with professional retouching before being animated. I helped lead the image side: keeping the look consistent from shot to shot, fixing textures that AI tends to get wrong, and polishing each frame by hand. We also integrated the product into the scenes so it looks shot, not pasted in.

Real products in a fantasy, food-inspired world.

This project focused on developing high-quality key visuals and product shots that integrate real products into a fantasy, food-inspired world. The goal was to combine the realism of product photography with the freedom of AI-generated environments, so that the final images feel like a single, cohesive piece rather than a composite.

Privacy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short

This website does not set cookies, has no analytics and does not ask you for personal data. It does not sell or share anything about you. It does load a few things from other companies, explained below.

Who is responsible

Mario Díaz Aguado (Mario Diz), Madrid, Spain — hey.mariodiz@gmail.com.

Visitor statistics

The site does not collect visitor statistics.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

3D library

The 3D tomato (the logo in the menu bar of every page, and the large one on the home page) and The room use the three.js library, which is loaded from the jsDelivr CDN. When you open any page of this site, your browser may connect to jsDelivr, which may log technical data such as your IP address. See jsDelivr’s privacy policy.

Embedded posts

The Lexus Electrified project page embeds a post from Instagram. When that page loads, your browser connects to Instagram (Meta), which may set cookies and process data according to its own policy. See Instagram’s privacy policy.

On your device

To work properly, the site remembers a few small things in your browser, such as where you were on the home page when you open a project and come back, and your Human or Machine view preference. They stay on your device and are never sent to me or to anyone else. Closing the tab removes temporary session settings. Your view preference stays until you change it or clear the site’s data.

Links to other sites

Some links lead to other websites (for example, a file on Google Drive in The room). Once you leave this site, their own privacy policies apply.

Contacting me

If you write to me by e-mail, I use your message only to reply. Ask me at any time to delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete any personal data I hold about you. Write to hey.mariodiz@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD.
