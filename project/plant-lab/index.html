Procedural drawing, creative code

Plant Lab is a drawing machine for plants. Every seed grows a different one, and every line of it is drawn by code: there is no image, scan, texture or font behind the drawings.

The human quality of the strokes is entirely procedural. The tremor of the hand, the pressure that fades at the end of a line, the grain of the pencil, the fibres of the paper and even the handwriting of the names are generated by noise and mathematics, never traced, scanned or drawn by hand.

The sandbox is built from nodes. Connect a seed to a plant and a plant to a sheet, and change the medium, the ink and the paper in between. Add nodes to fix a trait, turn the plant, draw a sheet of specimens or compare every variant of one trait.

Everything is drawn in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
