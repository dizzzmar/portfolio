ENMAD is a design school that doesn’t exist yet: an international centre for 2032, inside Nave 15 at Matadero Madrid, for designers who learn the rules so they can break them. Its line says it all: es un placer romper contigo, it’s a pleasure to break up with you.

It was my final project in Integral Design and Image Management, and I did all of it: the identity, the building and the brand strategy.

Graphic identity

A maximalist identity made of shapes that look torn apart: a logo with three variations, a loud palette and a kit that covers the facade, banners, signage, merch, posters and the website.

Spatial design

The shapes of the identity were laid on the floor plan of Nave 15 and then peeled off, like torn paper, to become curved roofs. Underneath them: studios, workshops, gardens, a canteen and rooms to stay, with no two spaces alike.

Brand management

Mission, audience and the launch campaign: posters, social media, street actions around Matadero and an opening event, all under the same break-up line.

University project, Integral Design and Image Management, 2022–2023. ENMAD is a fictional school; any brands shown are not affiliated.
