Soap & Glory — Vanillalicious

About

Real products in a fantasy, food-inspired world.

This project focused on developing high-quality key visuals and product shots that integrate real products into a fantasy, food-inspired world. The goal was to combine the realism of product photography with the freedom of AI-generated environments, so that the final images feel like a single, cohesive piece rather than a composite.
