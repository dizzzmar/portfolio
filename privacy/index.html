Privacy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short

This website does not set cookies, has no analytics and does not ask you for personal data. It does not sell or share anything about you. It does load a few things from other companies, explained below.

Who is responsible

Mario Díaz Aguado (Mario Diz), Madrid, Spain — hey.mariodiz@gmail.com.

Visitor statistics

The site does not collect visitor statistics.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

3D library

The 3D tomato (the logo in the menu bar of every page, and the large one on the home page) and The room use the three.js library (MIT licence). It is hosted on this same site, so loading it does not involve any other server.

Embedded posts

The Lexus Electrified project page embeds a post from Instagram. When that page loads, your browser connects to Instagram (Meta), which may set cookies and process data according to its own policy. See Instagram’s privacy policy.

On your device

To work properly, the site remembers a few small things in your browser, such as where you were on the home page when you open a project and come back, or the background you pick on the machine page. They stay on your device and are never sent to me or to anyone else. Closing the tab or clearing the site’s data removes them.

Links to other sites

Some links lead to other websites (for example, a file on Google Drive in The room). Once you leave this site, their own privacy policies apply.

Contacting me

If you write to me by e-mail, I use your message only to reply. Ask me at any time to delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete any personal data I hold about you. Write to hey.mariodiz@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD.
