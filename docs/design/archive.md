# Design detail: Digital Archive and its collections

Detail for this area of the design system. `DESIGN.md` holds the system and a summary; read this file only when working on this area, and keep new per-page detail here, not in `DESIGN.md`.

- **Digital Archive (/archive/, /archive/fruit-stickers/):** page black with cream text, like the Pokémon album. The index is a huge «Digital Archive» title with a 20 px subtitle beside it, at the right, its last line on the title's baseline (14 px and under the title on a phone; «Inspired by my friend Nicolò Lombardi’s digital archive», with a link to nicolo-lombardi.com/archive/ that opens in a new tab; under the title on a phone) and one row per collection (name at up to 72 px with its count; no thumbnails; no horizontal lines between the rows: they are separated by space, 12–24 px above and below each name; the black part always fills the screen (`min-height: 100svh`), so the red footer is only seen when you scroll down, on both this index and the Playground menu). A collection is an endless wall in the same skewed lattice as the Pokémon album (same engine, square pieces at 1.1× size), with no captions or any text: the name of each piece lives only in its alt text. Pressing a piece (or Enter) opens its viewer, as in the reference: a black screen with the name huge at the top (words rise out of their masks), the piece at the centre and its facts in ruled rows at the bottom (shown only when the piece has them), with «Close» at the top right (also Esc). The piece arrives with a bright flash that fades (1.1 s) and tilts up to 20° towards the pointer wherever it is on the whole screen, not only over the piece, with a soft highlight following it (on touch, while dragging); reduced motion removes the tilt, the flash and the rises. The address carries the piece (`#sticker-03`), so it can be shared and Back closes the viewer. A black gradient under the fixed bar keeps it legible while pieces pass beneath. In the menu, «Digital Archive» sits below the five big links as a smaller line (24 px on a phone, last in the column). The pieces are scans of real stickers cropped to their shape (Pink Lady, Plátano de Canarias so far), each at its own proportions inside a square cell.
- **Fave album covers (/archive/fave-album-covers/):** second collection, same structure as Fruit stickers (square covers instead of cut-out stickers; the viewer lists Artist, Album, Year and Designer). Eleven covers so far (Lost Weekend, Rubber Soul, Sgt. Pepper, Beatles 1, Ram, Romance, Dopamine Chamber, McCartney, Ambient 1, Music for Nine Post Cards, Minecraft – Volume Alpha), kept at their own resolution (some are small: 180–320 px, and the covers in this collection are not all square (the wall uses each file’s own proportions), so they look soft in the viewer; replace them with larger files in `assets/archive/fave-album-covers/` when available). In the viewer the piece is sized against the space left under the title, so long titles never overlap the facts; titles over 20 characters are set smaller (max 88 px) and over 30 smaller still (max 64 px). Mario gives the cover; the album, artist, year and designer facts are looked up from public sources, and a missing credit shows «—» (edit `items.json`).
- **Pixel trail (Digital Archive: index and both walls, in white; Posters, in dark ink `#0c0c0b` on its light page, set with `data-trail="ink"` on `<body>`; the Posters page no longer has the dot-matrix background):** a trail of white round dots on a 240-column grid (tiny dots, 6 px cells at 1440 px) follows the mouse (React Bits «PixelTrail», ported to plain JavaScript in js/pixel-trail.js; mounted by js/archive-trail.js). Dots are tiny, about 3 px (gap 0.5 of a cell), they drop out in a dither pattern over 1 s, with no shine or glow and no click burst; one fixed full-screen canvas BEHIND the content (z-index -1: the trail passes under the pieces, covers and texts, never over them; `<html>` paints the page colour and `<body>` is transparent so it does not hide the trail), never catching clicks. Mouse only: not created on touch, with reduced motion, with data saver or without WebGL2 (then the page is as before). The values live at the top of js/archive-trail.js.
- **Back button (collection pages):** «Back» with a small left arrow sits in the fixed bar just right of the tomato (14 px, white at 70 %, white on hover with the arrow nudging 3 px left; it takes the bar's difference blend) and links to /archive/ (a real link, aria-label «Back to Digital Archive»). The piece viewer keeps its own «Close» and covers the bar.
