# Design detail: Digital Archive and its collections

**Typography and colour direction (7 Nov 2026):** `visual-language.md` governs future changes. Existing layout descriptions below record the current implementation; the two-voice typography and flexible palette rules have not yet been applied in CSS.

Detail for this area of the design system. `DESIGN.md` holds the system and a summary; read this file only when working on this area, and keep new per-page detail here, not in `DESIGN.md`.

- **Digital Archive (/archive/, /archive/fruit-stickers/):** page black with cream text, like the Pokémon album. The index has the same structure as «Selected projects» on the landing page: two columns, a third and two thirds, with the title «Digital Archive» on the left (up to 100 px, `clamp(40px, 5.2vw, 100px)`, sticky under the bar) with its 20 px credit under it («Inspired by my friend Nicolò Lombardi’s digital archive», with a link to nicolo-lombardi.com/archive/ that opens in a new tab; 14 px on a phone), and on the right one row per collection, as a block centred in the right-hand space from 761 px up, both horizontally and vertically (mid-height of the screen, the same space above and below), well apart from the title, with the names flush left (ragged right: they all start at the same left edge); the title stays at the top left; on a phone (up to 760 px) the page is a full-height column: the title at the top, up to 23 vw (120 px max) with its credit under it, and the collections at the bottom at thumb height, like the menu links, much larger (9.2 vw, 40 px max, with the count at 36 % of that), so the screen is not left empty and they are comfortable to press; desktop is unchanged (name at 22–40 px, `clamp(22px, 2.4vw, 40px)`, well below the title so that the title stands out, with its count; no thumbnails; no horizontal lines between the rows: they are separated by space, 12–24 px above and below each name; the black part always fills the screen (`min-height: 100svh`), so the red footer is only seen when you scroll down, on both this index and the Playground menu). A collection is an endless wall in the same skewed lattice as the Pokémon album (same engine, square pieces at 1.1× size), with no captions or any text: the name of each piece lives only in its alt text. The wall drifts very slowly on its own (about 8 px per second up and to the left, `drift` in js/archive-wall.js; it pauses while dragging, while a viewer is open and with reduced motion; the Pokémon album does not drift). Pressing a piece (or Enter) opens its viewer, as in the reference: a black screen with the name huge at the top (words rise out of their masks), the piece at the centre and its facts at the bottom as plain columns with no lines (small label above, the value below in 20–40 px Funnel Display 600, all flush left across the full width; two columns on a phone; shown only when the piece has them), with «Close» at the top right (also Esc, or pressing anywhere outside the piece; pressing the piece itself does nothing). Closing with the mouse leaves no focus ring on the piece; focus returns to it only when the viewer was opened with the keyboard. The piece arrives with a bright flash that fades (1.1 s) and tilts up to 20° towards the pointer wherever it is on the whole screen, not only over the piece, with a soft highlight following it (on touch, while dragging); reduced motion removes the tilt, the flash and the rises. The address carries the piece (`#sticker-03`), so it can be shared and Back closes the viewer. A black gradient under the fixed bar keeps it legible while pieces pass beneath. In the menu, «Digital Archive» sits below the five big links as a smaller line (24 px on a phone, last in the column). The pieces are scans of real stickers cropped to their shape (Pink Lady, Plátano de Canarias so far), each at its own proportions inside a square cell.
- **Fave album covers (/archive/fave-album-covers/):** second collection, same structure as Fruit stickers (square covers instead of cut-out stickers; the viewer lists Artist, Album, Year and Designer). Eleven covers so far (Lost Weekend, Rubber Soul, Sgt. Pepper, Beatles 1, Ram, Romance, Dopamine Chamber, McCartney, Ambient 1, Music for Nine Post Cards, Minecraft – Volume Alpha), in the viewer from the files Mario supplied, converted to WebP (long side up to 2000 px; sizes range from 500 px for McCartney to 2000 px for Sgt. Pepper and Dopamine Chamber; the smaller ones (McCartney 500, Lost Weekend 700, Beatles 1 721) can be replaced with larger files in `assets/archive/fave-album-covers/` when available). In the viewer the piece is sized against the space left under the title, so long titles never overlap the facts; titles over 20 characters are set smaller (max 88 px) and over 30 smaller still (max 64 px). Mario gives the cover; the album, artist, year and designer facts are looked up from public sources, and a missing credit shows «—» (edit `items.json`).
- **Pixel trail (Digital Archive: index and both walls, in white; Posters, in dark ink `#0c0c0b` on its light page, set with `data-trail="ink"` on `<body>`; the Posters page no longer has the dot-matrix background):** a trail of white round dots on a 340-column grid (finer dots, about 4 px cells at 1440 px) follows the mouse (React Bits «PixelTrail», ported to plain JavaScript in js/pixel-trail.js; mounted by js/archive-trail.js). Dots are tiny, about 2–3 px (gap 0.5 of a cell), the brush is narrow (0.075 of the long side) and they drop out in a dither pattern over 0.8 s, with no shine or glow and no click burst; one fixed full-screen canvas BEHIND the content (z-index -1: the trail passes under the pieces, covers and texts, never over them; `<html>` paints the page colour and `<body>` is transparent so it does not hide the trail), never catching clicks. Mouse only: not created on touch, with reduced motion, with data saver or without WebGL2 (then the page is as before). The values live at the top of js/archive-trail.js.
- **Back button (collection pages):** «Back» with a small left arrow sits in the fixed bar just right of the tomato (14 px, white at 70 %, white on hover with the arrow nudging 3 px left; it takes the bar's difference blend) and links to /archive/ (a real link, aria-label «Back to Digital Archive»). The piece viewer keeps its own «Close» and covers the bar.

- **Think pieces (/archive/think-pieces/, one essay per folder):** the third entry of the Digital Archive index («Think pieces (1)»), for essays and articles Mario writes. Their format is raw on purpose: **white page, serif type (the system's Times, `--font-serif` in `css/essays.css`, no download) and all text in red** (the menu red `--menu-red`, #df1f1f: 4.8:1 on white). It is the only serif on the site and the only white page of the archive. The list page reuses the archive index layout (title «Think pieces» at the left, one row per essay on the right: the title in regular serif and the date under it; a long title wraps at 18 em and balanced; checked with nine essays on desktop and phone); an essay page (`/archive/think-pieces/<name>/`) is one left-aligned column of text, up to 34 em, with 18–22 px type at 1.5 line height (1.2 em between paragraphs) and the title and date above, with «Back» in the bar and the usual red big footer. **The top bar is red too** («Back» and «Menu» in `--menu-red`, no `difference` blend) and, unlike the other pages, sits on a flat white background so the red text of the essay does not pile up behind it when it scrolls underneath. No pixel trail and no pieces. For now there are two essays (newest first): **«La esencia de lo háptico.»** (Jun 8, 2025; subtitle «Del gr. ἁπτικός haptikós, y este der. de ἁπτειν háptein 'tocar'.»; the opening lines of the song as a bold italic pull quote with its credit in `.pull`; the cover of Phoebe Bridgers' «Stranger in the Alps» with its italic caption, `assets/archive/think-pieces/la-esencia-de-lo-haptico.webp`; it names Mario's grandparents Pili and Vicente) and the first think piece, **«Una oda a la nostalgia.»** (May 31, 2025; in Spanish, `lang="es"` on the article, with the subtitle «Saudade: (voz gallega) f. Soledad, nostalgia, añoranza», a photo of a toddler in a red sweater holding up sunglasses (`assets/archive/think-pieces/una-oda-a-la-nostalgia.webp`, 1800 px) and the text, with the three lines of verse in italics); it was first published on Mario's Substack (mariodiz.substack.com) and copied here with its photo (no link to or request from Substack). The pages are indexed (in the sitemap, `llms.txt` and `machine/`). The site is in English but the essays can be in any language: set `lang` on the article. To publish another essay: copy `archive/think-pieces/una-oda-a-la-nostalgia/` with another name, write the text (each `<p>` is a paragraph, `<p class="verse">` an italic quote, an image is a `<figure><img></figure>` inside `.essay-text`), add its `<li>` to `archive/think-pieces/index.html` (newest first), raise the count in `archive/index.html` and add the page to `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt` and `machine/index.html`.

- **Fruit stickers is locked («coming soon»):** in the Digital Archive index its row is not a link (a `<span class="archive-row is-soon" aria-disabled="true">`, not focusable, no cursor picture) and shows «(coming soon)» next to the name in place of the count; it is dimmed to 55 % (cream on black, over 6:1). The collection page `/archive/fruit-stickers/` still exists but is hidden: `noindex`, and out of the sitemap, `llms.txt` and `machine/`. To open it: put back the `<a class="archive-row" href="/archive/fruit-stickers/" data-cursor-pic="random:/assets/archive/fruit-stickers/items.json">` with `(2)`, remove the `noindex` and restore it in the sitemap, `llms.txt` and `machine/index.html`.
- **Miniaturas en la rejilla:** cada pieza de `items.json` puede llevar `"thumb"` (WebP de 512 px, `<nombre>-thumb.webp`). La rejilla carga la miniatura y el visor la imagen grande (`js/archive-wall.js`). Al añadir una portada nueva, crea también su miniatura (si falta, se usa la grande).

## Typography scope (revised)

Source Serif 4 is reserved for the Think pieces index and its essays. The main Digital Archive, collection titles and viewers, and the Room heading use Funnel as before. About stays Funnel. Only Think pieces loads `css/personal-type.css`; its dates, captions, navigation and controls remain Funnel. Palettes and layouts are unchanged by this typography correction.

## Shared page structure (7 November 2026)

Projects, About, Archive and the essay index share heading scale, outer gutters, heading gaps and column spacing via `css/page-structure.css`. The existing type voices and palettes remain. Projects’ identity line scrolls with content rather than occupying fixed navigation. Archive category lists align to the top of the right column on desktop and follow the introduction with a bounded gap on mobile; their previous bottom-of-viewport placement is superseded. About retains its portrait/content composition, with content gutters aligned to the shared page margins. Project case-study layouts and functional Back links are unchanged.


## Shared media controls

`css/media-controls.css` gives Posters, Archive, Pokémon and project video controls the same 44 px minimum targets, Funnel labels, focus outline and Close alignment. Existing viewer layouts, palettes, escape behaviour and collection gestures are preserved. Video controls expose sound state and readable playback position, update the fullscreen action on entry/exit, and reset seeking on pointer cancellation. The shared player still keeps native controls when JavaScript is unavailable; unsupported fullscreen controls are hidden.

## Shared motion rhythm

`css/motion.css` defines 200 ms viewer fades (150 ms with reduced motion), 450 ms content entrances and a 16 px arrival distance. Native gallery dialogs share cancellable fades via `js/viewer-motion.js`; Archive uses the same timing. About now arrives with one quiet fade rather than separate sliding layers. Archive’s bright opening flash and delayed metadata are removed. Artwork tilt, infinite gallery movement, the tomato, Room and the shared page/menu transitions keep their distinct functions. Functional labels remain Menu/Close/Back, Play/Pause, Sound and Full screen; the player changes its action to Exit full screen while expanded.
- **Filas del índice más pequeñas (nov 2026):** `clamp(18px, 1.7vw, 28px)` (antes hasta 40 px) y en móvil `min(6.4vw, 28px)`. El título del índice no cambia: lo manda `--page-heading-size` (css/page-structure.css), igual que Projects y About. Lo comparten Digital Archive, Think pieces y el menú de Playground (oculto).
