# Design detail: Chrome: menu, bars, links, footer, cursor, sounds

**Typography and colour direction (7 Nov 2026):** `visual-language.md` governs future changes. Existing layout descriptions below record the current implementation; the two-voice typography and flexible palette rules have not yet been applied in CSS.

Detail for this area of the design system. `DESIGN.md` holds the system and a summary; read this file only when working on this area, and keep new per-page detail here, not in `DESIGN.md`.

### Navigation
- **Bar:** 64 px high, tomato logo at the left (24 px, 14 px on mobile), links right in 14 px white at 70 %. On every page with a bar the links are replaced by one word, **Menu**.
- **Menu:** the "Menu" word, right-aligned in the bar, opens a full-screen **Menu Red** panel with cream text. The panel falls from the top like a curtain (0.6 s, `cubic-bezier(.77,0,.175,1)`); the five big links rise one by one out of a mask (0.7 s, 70 ms apart); the footer line fades in last. Closing is the same animation reversed and faster. The tomato and "Close" are drawn exactly where the page's tomato and "Menu" are, so the bar appears not to move.
- **Page transition (one for the whole site: links, menu, terminal, the room, About and the 404): stacked sheets, like layers in an app.** Going forward the new page rises from the bottom as a sheet with rounded top corners (28 px, flattening as it lands) while the old page stays underneath, shrinks to 0.94, lifts 3.5 % and dims to 55 % (a dark `#0c0c0b` shows at its edges). Going back it is the reverse: the top sheet drops away and the page below recovers size and light. «Back» is the browser's back button (history entry earlier than the current one) or arriving at the landing page (the bottom layer); everything else is «forward» (`js/transitions.js` marks it with a view-transition type, `back` or `forward`, and `css/transitions.css` animates it). One curve for everything, `cubic-bezier(.32, .72, 0, 1)` (an iOS-drawer curve: starts fast, settles slowly), 0.38 s in total; only transform, clip and brightness are animated. The tomato in the bar stays put. Reduced motion: a plain 150 ms fade. Each page keeps its own entrance animations, which play live on the new sheet. Replaces the colour curtain of PR #357.
- **Choosing a link to another page:** the panel does not wait: the page changes at once (about 0.15 s after the click, it used to wait 0.56 s) and the panel, still open, is what the curtain takes away. Links that stay on the page (Work on the landing, Contact's envelope, Terminal) close the panel first.
- **Page preloading:** every page carries a `<script type="speculationrules">` in its head (the browser's own feature, no third party, ignored by Firefox/Safari that do not support it). The six menu destinations (About, Archive, Playground, FAQ, Room, Posters) are prefetched as soon as the page loads and any other same-site link when the pointer rests on it, so the next page's HTML is already there when the curtain starts. It prefetches only (not prerender), so no page script runs before the visit. To add a main page, add it to the `urls` list in every page.
- **Big links:** Projects, About, Room, Digital Archive, Contact (Digital Archive took the place Playground had; the Playground page is hidden for now, see `docs/design/playground.md`). One row centred in the panel (on a phone, a column anchored 32 px above the bottom edge, set at 12.6 vw at most so «Digital Archive», the longest, fits on one line down to 320 px). Hovering one lowers the others to 75 % opacity (still 3:1); the current page is underlined.
- **No footer in the menu:** the panel carries no footer line. FAQs and Privacy live in the big footer (and The room keeps its own small line, as it cannot scroll). Machine mode, which has no menu, keeps its own Human | Machine switch.
- **Keyboard:** focus moves into the panel, Tab stays inside, Esc closes and focus returns to "Menu". With reduced motion the panel only fades (0.15 s).
### Navigation bars: no background, always «difference»
No page has a visible breadcrumb line any more (the pages still declare a `BreadcrumbList` in JSON-LD for search engines). Every bar (the home, the project pages and the `.nav` of About, FAQs, Privacy, Room and the Digital Archive) has no background and no gradient: content passes beneath it and the bar blends with it (`mix-blend-mode: difference`, white text): white on black, black on white. On the green pages the white text turns lilac over the green. The tomato sits outside the `<nav>` so its colours are not inverted. The studies pink and the pages with their own palette keep a normal bar (`body.studies-on`, `[data-palette]`). A new page with a `.nav` gets this from `css/nav-links.css` as long as the logo is placed before the `<nav>`.
### Text links
No fixed underline and no line on hover: on hover, as on donut-studio.com, the letters of a text link rise one by one and the word is written again from below (0.42 s, 20 ms between letters, `var(--ease-out)`; css/link-roll.css, js/link-roll.js). It covers the bar, the footer, the menu (small and big links) and the links inside paragraphs; project cards, thumbnails, logos and player controls keep their own hover. In a paragraph there is also a faint permanent line. On touch there is no roll: a normal underline. The current page keeps the full line. Reduced motion: no roll.
- **Link sounds (all pages, on by default):** a very soft «tick» (about 25–45 ms) when the mouse enters a link or button and a short «click» when it is pressed (js/sounds.js, three Kenney «Interface Sounds» files, CC0, in assets/sounds/ with an .m4a copy for browsers that cannot play .ogg). The tick alternates between two sounds and every play shifts the pitch randomly by ±6 %; volume is low (tick 22 %, click 32 %). It is on until the visitor turns it off with «Sound on / Sound off», the last link of the big footer (same slash and uppercase as its links; a real button with aria-pressed), and the choice is kept in this browser only (localStorage `dizmar-sound`). Mouse only (nothing on touch, and no switch is shown there); browsers do not allow sound before the first click on each page (the sound data is fetched while the browser is idle and decoded on that click), so ticks start after it and the very first click may be silent. Every page loads it except `machine/` (the plain-text mode stays silent) and the page template. Pages without the big footer (The room, the archive walls) keep whatever the visitor chose elsewhere but have no switch.
- **Image cursors in the Digital Archive index:** over the subtitle («Inspired by my friend Nicolò Lombardi’s digital archive») the yellow dot disappears and the cursor becomes Nicolò's favicon (an anime eye, a local copy at `assets/archive/nicolo-eye.webp`, 56 px max, so no request goes to his site); over the «Fruit stickers» row it becomes a random sticker of the collection, up to 120 px (a different one, with a different tilt of up to ±14°, each time the pointer enters). The image sits outside the dot so its colours are not inverted by the difference blend, arrives with a small scale-in (0.4 s) and shrinks to 0.88 on press; the system hand is hidden there. Any element can do it with `data-cursor-pic="/path/image.webp"` or `data-cursor-pic="random:/path/items.json"`; mouse only, off on touch (js/cursor.js, css/cursor.css). The images are preloaded (idle, right after the page opens; skipped with data saver) and the dot only steps aside once the image is ready, so there is never a moment without a cursor.
- **Big footer (the only footer, same on every page that scrolls):** a flat block in the menu red with «DIZMAR» huge in cream and bold (Funnel Display 800), fitted edge to edge by script; a row of links at the top right (FAQs, Privacy, Digital Archive, LinkedIn, each with a short slash before it, 14 px uppercase) and, along the bottom, «Mario Diz, Madrid» on the left and the «Go up» button on the right (a 56 px outlined square with an up arrow that fills cream on hover; it replaces «Back to top»). Reference: wearepanico.com. Its pieces (links, letters, button, line) arrive scattered, displaced and rotated by fixed amounts (letters travel 1.5× further; 0.55× on a phone) and settle into place as the block scrolls in, tied to the scroll (progress from the block's top entering the screen to its bottom reaching the bottom; `power1.out`), transform only, no library (`js/big-foot.js`, `css/big-foot.css`). At the bottom of the page «Go up» pulses once (0.9 s). Reduced motion: everything is already in place. It replaces the small footer line everywhere (About, FAQs, Privacy, Posters, every project, Playground and the Digital Archive index); The room (no scroll) and the full-screen wall of a collection have none. Pink (Studies) above and red below on the home is the combination to keep.
- **Favicon:** a solid circle in a vivid red (#ff2d1f, brighter than the menu red), on transparent for the tab icons (`favicon.ico`, `assets/images/favicon-192.png`) and on white for the home-screen icon (`apple-touch-icon.png`, which iOS would fill black if transparent).

- **Zonas táctiles (auditoría nov 2026):** en móvil los enlaces del footer grande miden 44 px de alto (`.bf-links a`); los enlaces sueltos («FAQs →», «Write to me →», «← Back to the portfolio») usan `padding-block` con `margin-block` negativo para llegar a 44 px sin moverse.

## Shared page structure (7 November 2026)

Projects, About, Archive and the essay index share heading scale, outer gutters, heading gaps and column spacing via `css/page-structure.css`. The existing type voices and palettes remain. Projects’ identity line scrolls with content rather than occupying fixed navigation. Archive category lists align to the top of the right column on desktop and follow the introduction with a bounded gap on mobile; their previous bottom-of-viewport placement is superseded. About retains its portrait/content composition, with content gutters aligned to the shared page margins. Project case-study layouts and functional Back links are unchanged.
