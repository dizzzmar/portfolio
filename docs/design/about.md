# Design detail: About: layout and the back of the portrait (flip scene)

Detail for this area of the design system. `DESIGN.md` holds the system and a summary; read this file only when working on this area, and keep new per-page detail here, not in `DESIGN.md`.

- **About (restored to the 28 Oct design, «foto grande y presentación a dos columnas»):** green page with lime text. On desktop the holographic portrait stays still on the left (sticky, up to 40 vw wide, 4:5, it still tilts to the mouse with a moving shadow) and the text card sits on the right (from 46 vw) and scrolls with the page: the huge «Mario Diz» title (Funnel Display), the biography in bold across both columns, two columns below (Art direction & visual design · Values), «Now playing» with the two covers, LinkedIn and the email under the two columns, Selected clients (the logos) and the FAQs link; then the big footer. The card enters from the right on a direct load (0.8 s) and each block fades up as it appears. On a phone the photo stays on top (sticky) and the card passes over it. The email shown is the current one (contact@dizmar.xyz, not the old Gmail one of that version) and the breadcrumbs and the small footer of that version are not back (the big footer replaces it). The images keep the mosaic load (js/pixel-load.js, `.px`; it measures the image through an ImageBitmap because with srcset + sizes `naturalWidth` is density-corrected and the crop came out magnified): each starts as an embedded 10-pixel-wide mosaic and sharpens in six steps when it arrives and is in view; `<html>` gets `fx` only when IntersectionObserver exists and reduced motion is not requested. The Locomotive-style version (4 Nov, PR #348) is in the history if it is ever wanted back.
- **About · the back of the portrait (a small living world):** clicking (or Enter/Space on) the photo turns it over in 0.9 s (rotateY, ease-in-out; instant with reduced motion) and shows a floating isometric pixel-art island in the spirit of x.com/daniildesigns' farm game: volume and several levels (cliffs with soil and rock strata, hanging rock blocks below, a raised terrace with a wooden fence and stone stairs), a cottage (lime plaster, timber frame, tiled roof, chimney with smoke, window boxes, porch bench, firewood), a woodshed with a chopping block, two raised vegetable beds and a water barrel, a pond with two ducks and shore stones that flows down a stream under a wooden bridge and falls off the cliff as a waterfall, beehives and a lookout bench on the terrace, a campfire with log seats, two street lamps, the outdoor office (two desks, whiteboard, coffee table, bench), trees built from small cubes like pixel art (oaks, round trees, stepped pines; no low-poly facets) that sway, cube bushes with a few blooms, rocks, mushrooms, hundreds of flowers and an orange cat. Three people (each with a strong colour, their own clothes, hair and umbrella; jointed at hips, knees, shoulders, elbows, waist and neck) live there and choose what to do from their needs (energy, social, focus, fun, nature, food), their character (red: work, coffee and wood; yellow: garden, bees, ducks and views; white: chats, campfire, stars, fishing and the cat, and the night owl), the hour and the weather, and they avoid what they just did, so it never repeats: type at the desks, explain at the whiteboard, coffee, read or check the phone on a bench, sit, stretch or do yoga on a hill, water or weed the garden, chop wood and carry a log to the house, watch the bees, fish from the bridge or the bank (with bites and ripples), feed the ducks, enjoy the view from the terrace or the cliff edge, rest against a tree, phone calls, chat (with a wave), pet the cat, sit at the campfire roasting marshmallows, lie on the terrace looking at the stars, sit on the porch, shelter under the shed, or go inside. At night they go home one by one and sleep (lit windows, then Zzz from the window); now and then one cannot sleep and comes out for a while. In the rain they open umbrellas, stop outdoor work and shelter. Time follows Madrid (today's sunrise and sunset): the sun crosses the sky with long shadows at dawn and dusk; dusk is warm, night is moonlit with lamps, fireflies and a lit campfire. Weather: clear, cloudy (puffy pixel clouds made of cubes, flat underneath, drifting and casting shadows) or gentle rain (rain streaks, ripples, wetter ground), and by default it changes by itself every few minutes. The background is set by the scene: white on the card and pink (#ffa2d4) full screen by day, tinted at dawn and dusk, greyer with clouds, dark at night. It is a real three.js scene (js/about-scene.js) rendered at low resolution (each scene pixel is 1.5 to 3 screen pixels: about 260 wide on the card, 560 full screen) and enlarged without smoothing; everything is drawn in code (no external images), static parts are merged into a few meshes, flowers are instanced, shadows are 1024 px on the card and 2048 full screen. It loads only on the first flip (prefetched on hover), only animates while it is turned and on screen, and stays still with reduced motion. The whole card tilts with the holographic foil as before; full screen it stays flat. On the turned card (desktop and phone alike), clicking the island makes the card grow to fill the window (0.5 s, ease-in-out; page scroll locked); clicking outside the island brings it back, and clicking outside the island on the card turns it back to the photo. **Full screen:** a frosted panel bottom left (full width on phones) sets Time of day (Morning, Daytime, Sunset, Night, with a «Madrid time» switch that follows the real hour) and Weather (Clear skies, Cloudy, Gentle rain, with «Let it change»); its × hides it into a small round button. The wheel or a pinch zooms towards the pointer (×1 to ×4.5), dragging pans (kept within the island), a double click or double tap zooms in or back out; keyboard: + and − zoom, arrows pan, Escape steps back. The cursor pill reads «Flip», «Open», «Zoom» or «Drag» over the island, and «Close» outside it.
